# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Someone at a desktop with video files already on disk. They open Tessel to assemble a short 2×2 or 3×3 mosaic, not to edit a timeline. The job on every screen is the same: place clips, set output, export one MP4.

## Product Purpose

Tessel is a local Electron app that combines 1–9 videos into a 2×2 or 3×3 H.264 mosaic. Empty cells stay black. Shorter clips pad to the longest (black or freeze). Success is a player-compatible MP4 saved where the user chose, with the grid still filled so they can tweak and export again.

## Positioning

Drop clips, get one mosaic MP4 — encoded on this machine, never uploaded.

## Brand Personality

Geometric, compact, direct. The block wordmark and the 450×600 window are the same idea: tessellated units, no chrome. Voice is instructional and short (“Select Videos”, “Convert”). The tool should disappear into the drop-and-export task.

## Anti-references

Not a nonlinear editor (no timeline, no trimming UI). Not a cloud mosaic or “upload your clips” web app. Not a photo collage maker. Not a SaaS dashboard or marketing landing.

## Design Principles

- The grid is the work surface; everything else is secondary.
- Stay on the machine. No account, no upload, no network required for the job.
- One primary action: Convert. Advanced settings stay out of the way until asked for.
- Familiar desktop controls over invented affordances.
- Incomplete layouts are valid: empty cells and mixed durations are part of the product, not errors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No WCAG level is claimed. The window is dense and keyboard-operable (focus-visible rings, labeled icon buttons, native dialog, combobox listboxes). Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep body text and controls at high contrast on the navy surface; do not mute labels into low-contrast gray.
